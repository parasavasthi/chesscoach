# Example Queries

## Recent game review

```text
My Chess.com username is <username>. Review my recent rapid games and find the top three recurring mistakes.
```

```text
Look at my latest blitz games and tell me whether I lose more from tactics, openings, endgames, or time trouble.
```

## Opponent analysis

```text
My opponent's Chess.com username is <opponent>. What patterns should I prepare for before playing them?
```

```text
Compare my public stats with <opponent> and tell me where I should try to steer the game.
```

## Opening analysis

```text
Check my recent games as Black and tell me which openings are giving me the worst positions.
```

```text
Find repeated early-game problems in my recent White games.
```

## Rating trends

```text
Use my Chess.com stats to explain why my blitz rating may be lower than my rapid rating.
```

```text
Look at my public stats and tell me which time control I should focus on improving first.
```

## Study advice

```text
Turn my recent-game patterns into a seven-day training plan.
```

```text
Give me a 30-minute daily routine based on my biggest weakness.
```

```text
Explain the main mistake pattern like I am a 1200-rated player.
```
