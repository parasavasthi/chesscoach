# Example Queries

## Public profile and stats review

```text
My Chess.com username is <username>. Review my public stats and tell me what I should study first.
```

```text
Look at my Chess.com ratings and records. Am I stronger in rapid, blitz, bullet, or daily?
```

## Opponent analysis

```text
My opponent's Chess.com username is <opponent>. What can I learn from their public profile and stats before playing them?
```

```text
Compare my public stats with <opponent> and tell me where I should try to steer the game.
```

## Archive planning

```text
Check which game archives are available for my username and tell me which month should be analysed first by the backend.
```

```text
I want a full review of my latest month. Which archive month should I send to backend analysis?
```

## Rating trends

```text
Use my Chess.com stats to explain why my blitz rating may be lower than my rapid rating.
```

```text
Look at my public stats and tell me which time control I should focus on improving first.
```

## Study advice

```text
Turn my public stats into a seven-day training plan.
```

```text
Give me a 30-minute daily routine based on my likely biggest weakness.
```

```text
Explain the main improvement priority like I am a 1200-rated player.
```
